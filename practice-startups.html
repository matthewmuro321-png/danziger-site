Practice Areas

Startups & Venture Capital.

Counsel to founders and investors across the life of a company, from formation through exit. Building a company is a sequence of consequential decisions made under pressure, and many of them are legal ones whose effects compound over years: how founders divide equity, how the company protects its intellectual property, and how the cap table is managed through successive rounds.

Overview

Our startups and venture capital practice counsels founders and emerging companies through the arc of building a company, and it represents the investors who fund them. We bring the sophistication of an institutional practice to companies at every stage, together with the practicality that early-stage businesses require.

We understand that early-stage clients need counsel that is both excellent and efficient, that moves at the pace of a young company, and that gives founders clear answers rather than expensive equivocation. Because we also represent the angels, funds, and strategic investors who provide the capital, that dual perspective makes us better counsel to whichever side we serve.

What We Do

Formation and founder matters. We advise on entity selection and formation, structure the founder equity and vesting arrangements that protect the company and the founders alike, and put in place the intellectual property assignments that ensure the company actually owns what it is built on. We also establish the equity incentive plans, option grants, and governance that investors expect to see.

Financings. We document early-stage financings, including convertible notes and SAFEs and the seed rounds that follow, and lead priced equity rounds from Series A onward. We negotiate term sheets and counsel founders on the terms that matter most over the long run: valuation and dilution, board composition and control, protective provisions, and liquidation preferences. We manage the cap table so the company enters each round, and ultimately its exit, with a clean ownership structure.

Investor representation. For angel investors, venture funds, family offices, and strategic and corporate investors, we conduct diligence, negotiate term sheets and definitive documents, and structure investments to secure the economic and governance rights that protect the investment through follow-on rounds, later financings, and liquidity events.

Growth and ongoing counsel. A financed company still has a business to run. We draft the customer, vendor, licensing, and partnership agreements that generate and protect revenue, advise on employment and equity matters as the team expands, and counsel on intellectual property, data and privacy, and board matters. This work connects naturally to our broader general counsel capability.

Exits. We handle acquisitions of venture-backed companies, advise founders and management on the personal and economic terms of an exit, counsel investors on the realization of their returns, and prepare companies for the diligence and deal process that a sale or public offering demands.
