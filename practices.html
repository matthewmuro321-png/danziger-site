Practice Areas

Areas of practice.

Our practice spans the legal needs of businesses, investors, entrepreneurs, families, and institutions—from day-to-day counsel to transactions requiring specialized experience.

  1. 01

    Corporate Law & General Counsel

    The firm advises privately held companies, investors, executives, and business owners on corporate matters throughout the life of a business. Our work includes entity formation and governance, commercial agreements, financings, joint ventures, and other transactions, as well as the legal issues that arise in day-to-day operations. For many clients, we serve as outside general counsel, providing ongoing advice and coordinating with specialist or litigation counsel when needed.

  2. 02

    Private M&A

    We represent buyers and sellers in acquisitions and dispositions of privately held businesses. Our work spans the full transaction, including letters of intent, diligence, structuring, financing, negotiation of definitive agreements, closing, and post-closing matters. We approach each transaction with an understanding of both the legal issues and the commercial considerations that shape the deal.

    More on Private M&A →

  3. 03

    Startups & Venture Capital

    The firm advises founders, emerging companies, and investors at each stage of a company's development. We assist with formation, founder arrangements, equity compensation, convertible instruments, venture financings, governance, commercial agreements, and exit transactions. We also represent angel investors, venture funds, and strategic investors, giving us experience on both sides of the financing relationship.

    More on Startups & Venture →

  4. 04

    Art Law

    Danziger, Danziger & Muro has advised participants in the international art market for decades. The firm represents collectors, artists, galleries, dealers, museums, auction-market participants, financial institutions, and other organizations in acquisitions and sales, art-backed lending, intellectual property matters, disputes, and other commercial transactions. Our work has also included the establishment of cultural institutions in the United States and abroad.

    The firm and its attorneys have been recognized by Chambers High Net Worth and leading art publications for their work in art and cultural property law.

    More on Art Law →

  5. 05

    Brand, Licensing & Publicity Rights

    The firm advises individuals and businesses on the ownership, use, licensing, and protection of valuable names, brands, creative works, and other intellectual property. Our work includes trademark matters, licensing agreements, endorsements, rights of publicity, name-image-and-likeness arrangements, and related commercial agreements. Clients include founders, artists, talent, luxury businesses, and established brands.

    More on Brand, Licensing & Publicity →

  6. 06

    Real Estate

    We represent businesses, investors, owners, landlords, and tenants in a range of commercial real estate matters. Our work includes acquisitions and dispositions, financings, retail and commercial leasing, and real estate issues arising in broader corporate transactions. The practice has particular experience representing luxury retailers, restaurants, and other operating businesses in connection with their locations and expansion.

    More on Real Estate →

  7. 07

    Hospitality

    The firm advises restaurants, hospitality groups, chefs, hotel owners, landlords, and investors on the corporate and commercial matters that arise in the hospitality industry. We handle restaurant and commercial leases, management and consulting agreements, joint ventures, licensing arrangements, financings, and capital raising. We also advise new and established hospitality businesses on their ongoing corporate needs.

    More on Hospitality →

  8. 08

    Executive Compensation & Employment

    We advise companies and executives on employment, compensation, and related workplace matters. Our work includes executive employment agreements, equity and incentive compensation, hiring and separation arrangements, restrictive covenants, confidentiality and trade-secret protections, and general employment counseling. These matters often arise in connection with the firm's corporate, M&A, hospitality, and closely held business practices.

    More on Executive Comp. & Employment →

  9. 09

    Family Office

    The firm advises family offices, families, and individual investors on corporate and transactional matters involving privately held assets. Our work includes the formation and governance of investment and holding entities, acquisitions and dispositions of businesses, art and real estate transactions, joint ventures, and other commercial matters. We also have substantial experience with cross-border matters, including those involving clients and assets in Japan and Korea.

    More on Family Office →

  10. 10

    General Counsel Services

    For companies that need senior legal judgment on a continuous basis without the cost of a full-time hire, the firm serves as outside or fractional general counsel. In that role it handles the recurring commercial agreements, governance, employment, and intellectual property questions every operating business generates, and manages specialist and litigation counsel when contested matters arise. Engagements are built around the needs of the particular business, from a defined monthly commitment to a discrete period of heightened activity.
