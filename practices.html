Practice Areas

Areas of practice.

The firm's principal practice areas are described below. Most client relationships extend across several practices and, in many cases, span decades.

  1. 01

    Art Law

    In 2016, artnet listed Danziger, Danziger & Muro, LLP as among "10 of the World's Most Powerful Art Lawyers." In 2015, Art + Auction listed the firm as among the 100 most powerful players in the art world. We advise buyers and sellers of major works; banks and borrowers on art lending; assist in settling complex art-law disputes; and quietly handle millions of dollars in transactions each year. The firm works with private collectors, artists, galleries, and various U.S. and foreign museums and educational institutions. Projects have included the establishment of the Isamu Noguchi Foundation in Mure, Japan, and the establishment of a sister museum of the Boston Museum of Fine Arts in Nagoya, Japan. In addition, the firm works closely with prominent clients in the world of fashion, including Issey Miyake, Ralph Rucci, and others, on matters of contract, copyright, trademark, and other intellectual property rights law. We also represent clients in the auction world on a variety of legal matters.

  2. 02

    Brand, Licensing & Publicity Rights

    A name, a likeness, and a mark are among the most valuable property a person or company can own. The firm counsels celebrities, talent, founders, luxury brands, and companies on trademark and brand licensing, endorsement and name-image-and-likeness deals, rights of publicity, and trademark protection and enforcement. The work involves structuring the agreements that capture a brand's value while preserving control of it, and it is closely connected with the firm's art, hospitality, and corporate practices.

    More on Brand, Licensing & Publicity →

  3. 03

    Corporate Law & Securities

    A substantial portion of the firm's practice involves corporate transactions: mergers and acquisitions, joint ventures, and financings. Our attorneys have represented many borrowers and lenders in secured and unsecured transactions, and have experience in bankruptcy and workout issues. The firm works with private equity funds and hedge funds, and their sponsors and managers, in product development, organization, formation, and the establishment and maintenance of appropriate operating procedures. We serve as outside general counsel to companies in a variety of industries, including luxury retail (jewelers, watch brands, clothing boutiques, and multi-brand holding companies), restaurateurs, medical device companies, and others.

    Danziger, Danziger & Muro, LLP has substantial experience in multi-state U.S. and international transactions, and the partners of the firm have established close working relationships with counsel and other professionals in many states and foreign countries.

    More on Corporate & Securities →

  4. 04

    Startups & Venture Capital

    We counsel founders and emerging companies from formation through exit, and we represent the angels, funds, and strategic investors who fund them. That work covers entity formation and founder equity, convertible notes and SAFEs, priced venture rounds, cap-table management, and the eventual sale or public offering. Representing both sides of the venture relationship makes us better counsel to whichever side we serve.

    More on Startups & Venture →

  5. 05

    Private Mergers & Acquisitions

    We represent buyers and sellers in the sale and acquisition of privately held companies, from term sheet through closing and beyond: sell-side and buy-side representation, diligence, deal structuring and financing, and the post-closing adjustments, earnouts, and indemnification matters that follow. Our objective is a closing that protects the client on terms that reflect the leverage they actually hold.

    More on Private M&A →

  6. 06

    Real Estate

    Danziger, Danziger & Muro, LLP continually works with clients on a wide variety of real estate transactions, including financings, acquisitions, and sales. We handle the retail and commercial leasing needs of many clients, including luxury retailers, restaurateurs, and other businesses.

    More on Real Estate →

  7. 07

    Hospitality

    DDM represents restaurant groups, celebrity chefs, hotel owners, landlords, and investors active in the food and beverage industry worldwide. We negotiate management agreements, consulting arrangements, and joint ventures between restaurateurs, owners, and hotel management companies. We also prepare and negotiate restaurant leases and licensing arrangements. Our practice includes structuring and executing fundraising for these projects. Finally, we work with start-up companies in the food and beverage industry, helping with their structuring, capital raising, and other general corporate needs.

    More on Hospitality →

  8. 08

    Executive Compensation & Employment

    The firm advises employers and executives on the agreements and decisions that define the employment relationship: executive employment and compensation, equity and incentive plans, hiring and workforce counsel, restrictive covenants and trade-secret protection, and separations and terminations. This work is closely connected with the firm's corporate, M&A, hospitality, and luxury-retail practices, where workforce questions are a constant.

    More on Executive Comp. & Employment →

  9. 09

    Family Office

    The firm advises family offices, multi-generational families, and ultra-high-net-worth individuals on the legal architecture of private wealth: entity formation and holding structures, acquisitions of art, real estate, and operating businesses, and the stewardship of long-held assets across generations and jurisdictions, including for international and Japanese and Korean families.

    More on Family Office →

  10. 10

    General Counsel Services

    For companies that need senior legal judgment on a continuous basis without the cost of a full-time hire, the firm serves as outside or fractional general counsel. In that role it handles the recurring commercial agreements, governance, employment, and intellectual property questions every operating business generates, and manages specialist and litigation counsel when contested matters arise. Engagements are built around the needs of the particular business, from a defined monthly commitment to a discrete period of heightened activity.
