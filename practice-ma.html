Practice Areas

Private Mergers & Acquisitions.

Counsel to the buyers and sellers of privately held companies, from term sheet through closing and beyond. The stakes are high, the timelines are compressed, and the difference between a clean closing and a costly dispute is written into the agreements negotiated in the weeks before signing.

On this page Overview What We Do Insights Transactions

Overview

The sale or acquisition of a privately held company is, for most of the people involved, among the most consequential transactions of their professional lives. We are deal lawyers in the truest sense: our objective is to get our clients to a closing that protects them, on terms that reflect the leverage they actually hold, without the delay and friction that put deals at risk.

We anticipate the issues before they surface, we keep the transaction organized and moving, and we never lose sight of the commercial result behind the legal task. When the negotiation is hard, we are firm, and when the deal calls for a practical compromise to get it done, we recognize it.

What We Do

Sell-side representation. We represent founders, families, private equity sponsors, and other owners in the sale of their businesses, managing the transaction from diligence preparation through closing and protecting the seller on the provisions that determine how much of the purchase price they actually keep: representations and warranties, the indemnification framework and its caps and survival periods, escrows and holdbacks, earnouts and post-closing adjustments, and restrictive covenants.

Buy-side representation. We represent strategic acquirers, private equity firms, and individual buyers, leading the legal due diligence that surfaces the risks a buyer must understand, advising on transaction structure and its tax and liability consequences, and negotiating the purchase agreement to secure the protections that matter, including roll-ups and add-on acquisitions designed to be repeatable and efficient.

Structuring, financing, and approvals. We advise on the choice among stock purchases, asset purchases, and mergers and the very different tax, liability, and consent consequences of each; structure equity rollover and management incentive arrangements; coordinate acquisition financing; and secure the third-party consents and approvals that condition closing.

Post-closing matters and disputes. We handle post-closing purchase-price adjustments and earnout calculations, indemnification claims and the disputes that arise from them, and the working-capital and reconciliation questions that surface after a sale. Because we draft the agreements with that possibility in mind, our clients enter those disputes on solid ground.
