Practice Areas

Executive Compensation & Employment.

Counsel to employers and executives on the agreements and decisions that define the employment relationship: who is hired and on what terms, how executives and key employees are paid and incentivized, what protects the company when an employee leaves, and how a separation is handled when the relationship ends. Each decision is far less costly to get right at the outset than to litigate later.

Overview

We advise companies on the corporate and transactional side of compensation, hiring, and separation, and we counsel executives on the agreements that govern their own careers. We bring the discipline of a corporate practice to a field where informal arrangements and outdated forms are a common and avoidable source of risk.

Our objective is to help employers structure compensation, hiring, and separation in a way that attracts and retains talent, protects the company's interests, and avoids the disputes that consume time and money. We do that with clear, practical counsel and agreements that hold.

What We Do

Executive employment and compensation. We negotiate and draft employment agreements, offer letters, and the compensation arrangements at the center of them: base and incentive compensation, bonus and commission structures, deferred compensation, and the change-in-control and severance protections executives expect and companies must structure with care. We counsel boards and compensation committees on executive pay, and we also represent executives in negotiating their own terms.

Equity and incentive compensation. We design and document stock option and restricted stock plans, profits interests, phantom equity, and other incentive structures, advising on grant terms, vesting schedules, and the tax considerations that determine whether awards achieve their purpose, as well as on the treatment of outstanding awards in financings and acquisitions.

Hiring, classification, and workforce counsel. We prepare offer letters and onboarding documents, advise on worker classification (employee vs. contractor; exempt vs. non-exempt), and draft the handbooks, policies, and codes of conduct that protect the company. We provide day-to-day counsel on leave, accommodation, discipline, and the wage-and-hour requirements New York and New York City impose.

Restrictive covenants and trade-secret protection. We draft confidentiality, non-solicitation, and non-competition provisions and assignment-of-inventions agreements, structure these covenants to be enforceable as the governing law changes, and counsel employers on protecting trade secrets when a departing employee threatens that protection.

Separations and terminations. We guide employers through individual terminations and reductions in force, draft separation and release agreements, and counsel on the timing, communication, and documentation that distinguish a clean separation from a contested one, bringing particular care to executive transitions.

Transactional employment matters. We handle the treatment of employees, executives, and incentive arrangements in acquisitions and financings, and structure the retention and incentive packages that keep key people through a transition, coordinating closely with the firm's corporate and M&A work.
