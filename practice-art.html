Practice Areas

Art Law.

Corporate and transactional counsel for the artists, dealers, collectors, and institutions that move the art market. The art world runs on relationships and discretion. It is also a market built on contracts, ownership, and risk, and behind every significant sale, consignment, loan, or commission lies a set of legal questions that can determine whether value is realized or lost.

Overview

Our art law practice serves the business side of the art market, advising artists, galleries, dealers, collectors, advisors, auction houses, foundations, and the businesses and investment vehicles built around art. We bring the discipline of a corporate and transactional practice to a field where deals are too often done on a handshake and a hope, and we structure those deals so that our clients are protected when the relationship is good and especially when it is not.

We approach art as both a passion and an asset, and we respect both dimensions. Our clients value lawyers who understand how this market actually works, who appreciate the sensitivities of dealing in objects of cultural and personal significance, and who can paper a transaction with precision without losing sight of the relationship behind it. That combination of commercial rigor and market fluency is what we offer.

What We Do

Sales, acquisitions, and consignments. We negotiate and draft purchase and sale agreements, consignment agreements, and private-sale arrangements, addressing the questions that determine whether a deal holds: title and warranties, authenticity and provenance, condition and conservation, payment and security, confidentiality, and the allocation of risk in the event of a defect or dispute. We represent both sellers disposing of works and collectors building collections, and we counsel dealers and galleries on the consignment and inventory arrangements that are the lifeblood of their businesses.

Galleries, dealers, and the art trade. We serve as counsel to the businesses of the art trade, handling entity formation and governance, artist representation agreements, primary and secondary market transactions, consignment and inventory financing, employment arrangements, and commercial leases. We advise on the regulations that increasingly affect art dealers, including anti-money-laundering and know-your-customer obligations, sales and use tax, and the import-export rules and cultural-property regimes that govern works crossing borders.

Artists and estates. We counsel artists, estates, and foundations on the corporate dimension of protecting and monetizing a body of work: gallery and representation agreements, commission and fabrication contracts, licensing and reproduction arrangements, copyright protection and the management of an artist's intellectual property, and the structuring of the estates and foundations established to steward an artist's legacy.

Art finance and investment. We structure and document art-secured lending and the related security arrangements, advise lenders and borrowers on the perfection and priority questions that art collateral presents, and counsel on the formation and operation of art investment funds, fractional-ownership platforms, and tokenized works.

Loans, exhibitions, and institutions. We represent museums, foundations, and private collectors in loan and exhibition arrangements. We negotiate loan agreements and the insurance, indemnity, transport, and condition provisions that surround them, and we counsel institutions on acquisitions, deaccessioning, and the governance and compliance questions that come with holding cultural assets in trust for the public.

Title, provenance, and risk. Questions of authenticity, provenance, and clear title are the fault lines of the art market. We conduct and advise on title and provenance diligence, structure warranties and representations to allocate the risk of a defect, and counsel clients on the authentication, restitution, and ownership questions unique to this field.

Recognition & Experience

Danziger, Danziger & Muro is one of the best-known firms specializing in art law. The firm has been ranked by Chambers HNW in Art and Cultural Property Law; partners Thomas C. Danziger and Charles T. Danziger were named by Art + Auction among the most powerful "Power Lawyers" in the art world, and the firm was listed by artnet as among "10 of the World's Most Powerful Art Lawyers."

Thomas and Charles Danziger are recognized authorities in the field. For more than a decade they wrote Art + Auction's widely read "Brothers-in-Law" column on legal issues in the art world, and they authored the chapter "The Illegal Art Trade" for Fine Art and High Finance: Expert Advice on the Economics of Ownership (Bloomberg Press, 2010). The firm's art-finance clients have included Bank of America, U.S. Trust, and Emigrant Bank Fine Art Finance.
