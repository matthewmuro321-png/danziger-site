Practice Areas

Brand, Licensing & Publicity.

Counsel to the people and companies whose names, faces, and marks are their most valuable assets. A name, a likeness, and a mark are among the most valuable property a person or company can own, and among the easiest to lose through a careless agreement or an unprotected right. We structure the trademark and licensing arrangements through which that value is protected and monetized, and we enforce the rights that make those agreements worth having.

Overview

Our brand, licensing, and publicity rights practice counsels the celebrities, talent, founders, brands, and companies whose identities drive commercial value. This is a field where the upside is enormous and the exposure is real: a well-structured license can build a brand across categories and generations, and a poorly drafted one can fracture a relationship, dilute a mark, or surrender control of a name.

We treat a client's brand and identity as the assets they are, and we bring to them the same rigor a sophisticated owner brings to any asset of consequence, drafting the agreements that capture the value while preserving the control.

What We Do

Trademark and brand licensing. We negotiate and draft trademark and brand-license agreements, merchandising and product-licensing deals, co-branding and collaboration arrangements, and the franchising and brand-extension structures through which a name moves into new categories and markets, with deliberate attention to scope and exclusivity, quality control and approval rights, territory and term, royalties and audit rights, and the termination and reversion terms that return a brand to its owner intact.

Celebrity, talent, and endorsement. We counsel celebrities, athletes, artists, founders, and other talent on endorsement and sponsorship agreements, brand-ambassador and spokesperson deals, name-image-and-likeness arrangements, appearance and licensing contracts, and the influencer and social-media agreements now at the center of many brand relationships. We also represent the brands and companies on the other side of those deals.

Rights of publicity. The right to control the commercial use of one's name, image, voice, and likeness is the legal foundation beneath every endorsement and identity deal, and New York's framework is among the most consequential in the country. We advise individuals and their representatives on protecting and licensing these rights, advise companies on securing the clearances lawful use requires, and counsel estates, heirs, and foundations on post-mortem publicity rights.

Trademark protection and enforcement. We counsel clients on the selection, clearance, registration, and portfolio management of trademarks, handle prosecution and maintenance, and police and enforce marks against infringement, dilution, counterfeiting, and bad-faith registration, including oppositions, cancellations, and the broader disputes that arise when rights collide.

Disputes. Identity and brand are contested assets, and disputes over them carry both commercial and personal stakes. We represent clients in matters involving trademark infringement and dilution, breach of license, right-of-publicity and misappropriation claims, false endorsement, and the ownership conflicts that surface when a founder's name and a company become entangled, always with attention to the reputational dimension that sets these matters apart.
