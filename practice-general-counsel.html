General Counsel Services.

There is a stage in the life of a growing company when ad hoc legal advice is no longer enough and a full-time legal department is not yet justified. We serve as the outside or fractional general counsel for companies that need senior legal judgment on a continuous basis, without the cost and commitment of a full-time hire.

Overview

This is a relationship, not a transaction. We invest the time to understand the business, its goals, its risk tolerance, and the people who run it, and we make ourselves a genuine part of the team. The result is counsel that is proactive rather than reactive, commercial rather than academic, and oriented toward keeping the business moving rather than slowing it down.

Companies engage us to be the steady legal presence that lets their leadership focus on building.

What We Do

As outside general counsel, we handle the recurring legal work that every operating business generates and that, left unattended, becomes a source of risk. We draft, review, and negotiate the commercial agreements that run through the company: customer and vendor contracts, master services agreements, licensing and distribution arrangements, nondisclosure agreements, and the form documents a business needs to operate efficiently and consistently.

We advise on corporate governance and maintenance, board and equity matters, employment questions across the life cycle, intellectual property protection, data and privacy obligations, regulatory compliance, and the day-to-day decisions where a quick and reliable answer prevents a future problem. When specialized or contested matters arise, we coordinate and supervise specialist and litigation counsel, and we support financings, acquisitions, and other significant transactions as the company-side counsel who keeps the deal organized and the company's interests protected.

How We Work

Our engagements are built around the needs of the particular business rather than a fixed template. Some clients retain us for a defined commitment of time each month, with the predictability of a flat fee and the availability of true in-house counsel. Others engage us to lead a discrete period of heightened activity, such as a financing round, a period of rapid hiring, or the build-out of a contract and compliance infrastructure. As a company grows, we scale with it, and when the time comes to bring legal in-house, we help structure that function and hand off the work cleanly.

We serve growth-stage and emerging companies that have outgrown founder-managed legal decisions, established businesses that prefer a flexible senior resource to a full-time hire, and companies in transition that need experienced legal leadership for a defined period.
