Family Office.

Counsel to family offices, multi-generational families, and ultra-high-net-worth individuals on the legal architecture of private wealth, from entity formation and holding structures through acquisitions, governance, and the stewardship of art, real estate, and operating assets across generations and jurisdictions.

What We Do

The firm's family office practice brings together its corporate, real estate, art, and international work in service of a single client relationship: the principal, the family, or the family office itself. Our work spans the structural (entity formation, holdings, governance) and the transactional (acquisitions of art, real estate, and operating businesses), and is often coordinated with the trusts-and-estates, tax, and overseas counsel chosen by the family.

How We Help

Family office structuring and governance. We advise families on the design and operation of their family offices, whether establishing a single-family office, converting an operating business into a wealth-management platform, or participating in a multi-family arrangement. We structure for tax efficiency and clear allocation of authority, and put in place the family constitutions, governance charters, and investment and conflict-of-interest policies that allow a family to make decisions in an orderly way.

Estate, trust, and succession planning. We design and implement comprehensive estate plans that move assets efficiently and privately while honoring the family's intentions, employing the full range of planning vehicles and coordinating across borders for families whose members and assets span more than one country. We also address the human dimension of succession, preparing the next generation for the responsibilities ahead.

Tax planning. We advise on income, gift, estate, and generation-skipping transfer tax at the federal and state level, including the questions that face New York residents and those changing domicile, and we coordinate closely with each family's accountants and tax advisors so that planning is integrated rather than fragmented.

Investments, businesses, and assets. We structure and document private investments, fund commitments, co-investments, and direct deals; counsel on the governance and transition of operating businesses held within the family; and advise on the acquisition, holding, and disposition of real estate, art, aircraft, vessels, and other significant assets.

Philanthropy. We establish and counsel private foundations, donor-advised funds, and charitable trusts, and structure major gifts and philanthropic ventures to achieve both the family's charitable goals and the associated tax benefits.

Confidentiality, protection, and fiduciary matters. We structure ownership and planning to safeguard confidentiality and insulate family assets, advise on prenuptial and marital agreements, counsel fiduciaries on the administration of trusts and estates, and represent clients in trust and estate disputes with the discretion these matters require.
