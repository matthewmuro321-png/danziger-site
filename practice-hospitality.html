Our hospitality practice is dedicated to delivering top-tier legal solutions to clients across the hospitality sector. Licensing & Regulatory

Few industries are as heavily regulated at street level as hospitality. We handle liquor licensing before the New York State Liquor Authority and comparable bodies in other jurisdictions, including new applications, transfers, alterations, and the defense of disciplinary proceedings. We also advise on health and safety compliance, sidewalk and outdoor-dining authorizations, cabaret and entertainment permits, and the layered municipal approvals that determine whether and how a venue can operate. For clients building brand systems, we structure franchise and licensing programs that comply with federal and state franchise law while preserving the operator's flexibility to grow.
