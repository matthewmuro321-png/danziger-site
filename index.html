The Firm

Founded in 1989, Danziger, Danziger & Muro is a New York firm dedicated to providing the highest quality of legal services to its clients. The principal practice areas of the firm consist of general corporate law, art law, real estate law, hospitality law, intellectual property law, and employment law.

Particular areas of concentration are the representation of luxury brands, members of the international art world, high-end restaurant and hospitality groups, family offices and ultra-high-net-worth individuals, and Japanese corporations and individuals. The firm has been ranked by Chambers HNW in Art and Cultural Property Law, named by artnet in 2016 as among the ten most powerful art lawyers in the world, and listed by Art + Auction in 2015 among the most influential players in the international art market.
