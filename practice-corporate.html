Emerging Companies & Venture Capital

From the first cap table to the exit, we advise founders, emerging growth companies, and the venture investors who finance them. A venture deal has two sides, and we know both of them cold: when we paper a founder's round, we have closed the same round from the investor's chair, and when we sit across from a fund, we have read the fund's documents from the inside. The result is counsel that anticipates the other side's position instead of reacting to it.

For founders and emerging companies. The decisions made in the first month are the most expensive to unwind: entity type, state of incorporation, founder vesting, the option pool, and a clean cap table from day one. We set founders up on the structure that institutional capital expects, help them raise on SAFEs and priced rounds without giving away what matters at the next round, and guide them through scaling, equity compensation, IP ownership, and eventual liquidity through M&A or the public markets.

For investors and funds. We form venture funds across the size and strategy spectrum, build the organizational documents that govern fee, carry, LP rights, and regulatory posture, and execute investments quickly without letting velocity become exposure. We support funds across the full arc: investment execution, regulatory and Investment Adviser Act compliance, portfolio and follow-on matters, and fund-level liquidity through secondaries, distributions, and orderly wind-downs.

Private Mergers & Acquisitions

We represent buyers and sellers in the sale and acquisition of privately held companies, among the most consequential transactions of our clients' professional lives. We are deal lawyers in the truest sense: our objective is a closing that protects the client, on terms that reflect the leverage they actually hold, without the delay and friction that put deals at risk.

Sell-side. We represent founders, families, and sponsors in the sale of their businesses, managing the transaction from diligence preparation through closing and protecting the seller on the provisions that determine how much of the purchase price they keep: representations and warranties, the indemnification framework and its caps and survival, escrows and holdbacks, earnouts and post-closing adjustments, and restrictive covenants.

Buy-side. We represent strategic acquirers, private equity firms, and individual buyers, leading the legal due diligence that surfaces the risks a buyer must understand, advising on transaction structure and its tax and liability consequences, and negotiating the purchase agreement to secure the protections that matter, including roll-ups and add-on acquisitions designed to be repeatable.

Structuring, financing & post-closing. We advise on the choice among stock purchases, asset purchases, and mergers; structure equity rollover and management incentives; coordinate acquisition financing and third-party consents; and represent clients in post-closing purchase-price adjustments, earnout calculations, and indemnification claims when they arise.
